# Simplify: cut the codebase by half

Decided by Nicolai on 2026-10-18. The repository is about 405k lines. The Rust
part is 257k lines, of which about 141k are tests and 42k are comment lines,
which leaves about 75k lines of code. The goal is to cut about 200k lines and
lose no feature anyone uses.

## Rules from now on

- Reviews block only on real correctness, privacy or data-loss bugs that someone
  can actually hit, with at most 2–3 rounds. A hypothetical interleaving goes into
  a note, not into a test or guard in the same PR.
- No new defensive guard without a reported failure behind it.
- A module header is 2–5 lines. Code comments name no plan IDs (P25, D5, A16b…)
  and add no notes on how the Swift app behaved.
- Tests cover real behaviour. As a rough cap, a crate's tests are about 0.6× its code.

## Kept

- Every feature: recording, transcription, diarization, summaries (including
  the Codex/ChatGPT provider), the Obsidian export, phone handover, the tray.
- Data-loss guards: recovery of an interrupted recording from its master,
  durable writes, the writer thread, AAC salvage of killed phone recordings (A14).
- Privacy: audio reaches the speech sidecar only over stdin, ONNX telemetry is off,
  and the handover uses TLS pinning.
- The store and its migrations, the bridge fixtures, the crash log file.

## Given up

- A recording can be lost when Linux kills Steno at logout or under the OOM
  killer, or when the audio folder sits on a NAS that acknowledges flushes it
  never writes.
- Windows updates become rougher at the edges (a declined consent prompt no
  longer restarts the old version).

There are no reports of any of these from real use. If one happens, it gets a
small fix aimed at that one case.
