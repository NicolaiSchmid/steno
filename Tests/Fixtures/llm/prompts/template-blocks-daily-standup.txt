Template: Daily Standup
A daily standup. Every bullet lead is the person's name: their known name, else the speaker label verbatim. Keep each person's update to what they themselves said and never merge two people's updates into one bullet.

Sections, in this order, with exactly these ids:
- progress-since-last-standup — "Progress Since Last Standup" (required): One bullet per person, led by their name, with what they finished or moved forward since the last standup.
- plans-until-next-standup — "Plans Until Next Standup" (required): One bullet per person, led by their name, with what they will work on until the next standup.
- blockers-and-help-needed — "Blockers and Help Needed" (optional, omit it when the meeting has nothing for it): Blockers, dependencies and requests for help, led by the person's name, with what or whom they are waiting on. Omit the section when there are none.
- announcements — "Announcements" (optional, omit it when the meeting has nothing for it): Team-wide announcements, decisions and schedule changes that are not one person's update.
