A general meeting between the participants. Write for someone who was not there and has to act on it: what was decided, what is open, what happens next. Prefer concrete facts, numbers, names and dates over general statements, and never invent anything that was not said.

Sections, in this order, with exactly these ids:
- executive-summary — "Executive Summary" (required): Three to five bullets with the outcomes that matter most: decisions, commitments, changes of direction, deadlines. The lead is a short noun phrase naming the topic; the text says what was concluded, not what was discussed.
- full-summary — "Full Summary" (optional, omit it when the meeting has nothing for it): One bullet per topic in the order it came up. The lead names the topic; the text gives the substance in one to three sentences and names who argued for or asked for what where that matters. Skip small talk and logistics.
- open-questions — "Open Questions" (optional, omit it when the meeting has nothing for it): Questions raised but not answered, and points explicitly deferred, one bullet each. The lead names the question in a few words; the text says who raised it and what depends on it.
